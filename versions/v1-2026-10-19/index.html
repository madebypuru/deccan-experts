09 FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about joining the network, the work, and how you get paid. Still curious? Email support@deccanexperts.ai.

01Is this network only for AI professionals?

No — we welcome experts from diverse fields and with a wide range of skills who can contribute to training and evaluating AI models.

02What skills are most valued for this network?

Analytical thinking, attention to detail, strong reasoning, writing ability, creativity, and critical thinking are highly valued. Specific requirements vary depending on the project type.

03What makes this network different?

Quality and impact. You'll work alongside the top 1% of talent, on high-stakes projects shaping AI worldwide, with continuous learning opportunities and reliable payout.

04How will I be paid, and how often?

Payments vary by project: some are task-based, others hourly, and a few use a combined model. All payment details, rates, and cycles are shared upfront. Payouts are processed biweekly.

05How soon will I get paid after completing work?

We run two payment cycles each month — Cycle 1 is processed during the first week (on or after the 2nd), and Cycle 2 during the third week (on or after the 17th).

06Whom can I contact if I have questions or issues?

A dedicated support team is available. You can reach out any time via email to support@deccanexperts.ai.
