Our Hiring Process

At Deccan AI, we believe in finding the right fit—not just for us but for you too. Here’s what to expect as you go through our hiring process

  1. 01

    Exceptional People

    Share your resume, portfolio, and a brief note about your interest in joining us.

  2. 02

    Introductory Call

    A 20 to 30 minute call to discuss your background and career goals while learning more about us.

  3. 03

    Technical / Role Assessment

    Complete a task or share past work to showcase your skills and problem-solving abilities.

  4. 04

    Interviews with the Team

    Engage with your potential manager and peers to discuss your expertise and fit for the role.

  5. 05

    Welcome Aboard

    You are in. Meet the team, get set up, and start shipping.
