Getting Started

Create your Account

Create your Deccan Experts account with passwordless email verification, then explore roles and track your applications.

Deccan uses passwordless email verification: enter your email to register and we send you a verification link. Signing in works the same way each time. You can also continue with your Google account.

Create your account

  1. 01

    Open the sign-in page

    Go to work.deccanexperts.ai/login. You’ll see a field for your email, Login, Sign Up and Continue with Google.

  2. 02

    Enter your email

    Enter your email address and select Sign Up. We send a verification email to that address.

  3. 03

    Verify from your inbox

    Open the email and follow the link to finish registering. Can’t find it? Check spam, or resend it.

  4. 04

    Fill in the basics

    Complete the required profile fields so you can apply for roles. You can add more detail later.

  5. 05

    Explore roles and apply

    Browse open roles and apply when you’re ready.

Signing in

  1. 01

    Go to the login page

    Enter your email and select Login. We send a one-time link each time you sign in.

  2. 02

    Open the email

    Open your inbox and select the login link in the message.

  3. 03

    Or continue with Google

    If you registered with Google, use Continue with Google on the same page.

Quick tips

  • Use an email address you can open straight away, so you can verify.
  • If a page looks stuck or out of date, hard-refresh it (Shift + Cmd/Ctrl + R).
  • Already have an account? Use Login on the same page.

FAQ

I only see a sign-in screen. How do I sign up?

Choose Sign Up on the same screen, enter your email and verify from your inbox. There’s no separate sign-up page.

Do I need a password?

No. Deccan uses passwordless email verification, so there’s no password to remember. Each sign-in sends you a one-time link.

Can I see roles before creating an account?

Yes. You can browse role categories first, but applying needs a verified profile.

I did not receive the verification email. What should I do?

Check your spam folder, then resend the email from the sign-in page. If it still doesn’t arrive, email support@deccanexperts.ai.

Can I use a work email address?

Yes, as long as you can open that inbox to verify each time you sign in.

Can I have more than one account?

Keep to one account. If you’ve already created one, use Login on the same page instead of signing up again.
